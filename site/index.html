Prologue · how this story is told

Each venture is a color. Together, a masterpiece.

We built Snapp Group's employer brand around one picture: a painter and a shared canvas. Every company brings its own color, and the canvas gets richer layer by layer. Here is my work, told the same way.

The scenes have buttons to press. or just scroll.
